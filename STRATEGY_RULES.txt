NQ ORB — OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT RULES
Version: 1.0
Specification status: User-specified research baseline; not yet backtested or validated.
Strategy family: Nasdaq-100 futures opening-range breakout.
Showcase instrument: Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 futures (MNQ).
All clock times: America/New_York (ET), with daylight-saving transitions handled correctly.

1. CORE STRATEGY

1.1 Opening range
- Use 15-minute bars.
- Build the opening range from the first 15-minute bar(s) after the 09:30 ET New York cash open.
- MNQ showcase preset: one bar, 09:30:00–09:45:00 ET.
- Opening-range high = highest high in the selected opening-range bar(s).
- Opening-range low = lowest low in the selected opening-range bar(s).
- Do not include pre-open bars or bars after 09:45 ET in the one-bar preset range.

1.2 Breakout signal
- After the opening range is complete, evaluate subsequent 15-minute bars in chronological order.
- Long breakout: the first eligible bar that closes strictly above the opening-range high.
- Short breakout: the first eligible bar that closes strictly below the opening-range low.
- A wick beyond the range without a close beyond it is not a breakout signal.
- The baseline is first-breakout-only: evaluate the first post-range closing breach. If either regime filter fails on that breakout bar, do not substitute a later breakout bar that day.
- Both long and short directions are enabled. At most one trade may be entered per trading day.

1.3 Regime filters — BOTH MUST AGREE
A breakout is eligible only when both filters pass on the breakout bar:
A. Relative volume (RVOL):
   - RVOL = breakout-bar volume / average volume of the preceding 20 completed 15-minute bars.
   - Exclude the current breakout bar from the average.
   - MNQ preset threshold: RVOL >= 1.0.
B. Volatility:
   - ATR percentage = ATR(14) / breakout-bar close × 100.
   - Use 15-minute bars and Wilder's ATR smoothing for the baseline.
   - MNQ preset floor: 0.10%.
   - MNQ preset ceiling: 0.60%.
   - Both bounds are inclusive.
- A breakout is rejected if either RVOL or ATR percentage is outside its allowed condition.

2. STOPS AND TARGETS

2.1 General stop choices
- The strategy may use either:
  A. the far side of the opening range (long stop at range low; short stop at range high); or
  B. an ATR-multiple stop.
- An optional maximum stop-distance cap in index points may be configured.
- Record the selected stop method and all stop parameters for every test.

2.2 MNQ showcase stop
- Stop method: ATR multiple.
- ATR length: 14 periods on 15-minute bars, using Wilder's smoothing.
- Long stop: entry price minus 0.4 × ATR(14) measured on the breakout signal bar.
- Short stop: entry price plus 0.4 × ATR(14) measured on the breakout signal bar.
- Stop-distance point cap: none.
- The far-side-of-range stop is not active in this preset.

2.3 Target
- Target distance = 0.4 × the initial entry-to-stop distance.
- Long target is above entry; short target is below entry.
- The 0.4 target multiple means a 0.4:1 reward-to-risk ratio (0.4R). Do not reinterpret it as 4.0R or 0.4 × ATR.
- Do not add trailing stops, scale-outs, or discretionary exits to the baseline.

3. POSITION SIZING — MNQ SHOWCASE

- Fixed planned stop-risk budget: USD 3,000 per trade.
- MNQ contract multiplier: USD 2 per one full Nasdaq-100 index point per contract.
- Minimum outright tick: 0.25 index points; tick value: USD 0.50 per MNQ contract.
- Initial contracts = floor(3,000 / (stop distance in index points × 2)).
- Round prices to valid ticks without improving the modeled fill.
- If the computed position size is less than one contract, skip the trade.
- The 3,000 USD amount is the planned price-to-stop risk before transaction costs. Report fees and slippage separately and also report all-in loss at the stop.
- Do not use NQ's USD 20-per-point multiplier for MNQ sizing.

4. SESSION AND TRADE LIFECYCLE

- No new entries from 15:00 ET onward. A breakout bar closing at or after 15:00 ET is ineligible.
- Flatten any open position at 15:55 ET.
- Only one trade per trading day, across both directions.
- Do not hold a position beyond the 15:55 ET forced flatten.
- Use the correct New York local date and daylight-saving rules when assigning a session.

6. EXECUTION AND BACKTEST CONVENTIONS THAT MUST BE EXPLICIT

The signal is known only after the breakout bar closes. Use next-15-minute-bar open as the primary executable entry assumption, with realistic adverse slippage. Also report signal-close execution as a separate sensitivity only; never mix the two assumptions.

Use 1-minute-or-better data for stop/target event ordering and the 15:55 ET forced flatten where available. Fifteen-minute OHLC bars cannot establish the exact 15:55 price or the order of multiple intrabar touches. If only 15-minute data can be sourced, clearly label the 15:55 exit and intrabar ordering as unverified; do not manufacture an exact fill.

If the same 1-minute bar touches both stop and target and no finer event sequence is available, resolve the ambiguity adversely (stop first). Include commissions, exchange/clearing fees, and slippage in net performance. Report the fee and slippage assumptions per contract and per side.

Use continuous futures carefully. Record the exact data symbol, contract month, roll method, adjustment method, data source, retrieval date, timezone, missing-bar policy, and file hashes. Do not let back-adjusted historical prices become simulated executable fills. Do not silently bridge roll gaps or mix contracts in the same volume average.

7. RESEARCH INTERPRETATION

- This file is the authoritative frozen baseline. Any change to an entry, filter, stop, target, sizing, time, direction, or trade-frequency rule is a separately versioned experiment and must not overwrite this specification.
- Report gross and net results separately.
- The 0.4R target has a theoretical pre-cost break-even win rate of 1 / (1 + 0.4) = 71.43%, assuming a full 1R loss and full 0.4R win. Costs and execution shortfall increase the required win rate.
- Do not describe a strategy as profitable without reproducible, cost-aware, out-of-sample evidence.
- This specification authorizes research and historical simulation only, not live trading.
